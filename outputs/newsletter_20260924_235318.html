AWS has expanded its developer ecosystem with the introduction of the Strands Harness. By decoupling the execution and tooling environment from the core LLM reasoning engine, AWS gives engineering teams the flexibility to swap underlying model brains without rewriting their orchestration logic. This decoupled design pattern is quickly becoming the gold standard for enterprise architectures seeking model neutrality.
